Privacy Policy - Reels Publisher

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Reels Publisher is a private tool used by the operator of the Loop Lab Facebook Page to upload and schedule videos on that Page. It is not offered to the general public.

Data we access

The app uses Meta's Graph API with authorization granted by the Page operator. It handles:

  • access tokens issued by Meta to the operator;
  • basic information about the Page, such as its ID and name;
  • information about the videos the app uploads, such as video ID, caption, schedule and publishing status.

The app does not collect personal data about people who watch, follow or interact with the Page.

How data is used

Data is used only to upload, schedule and check the status of the Page's own videos. It is not used for advertising or profiling.

Storage and security

Access tokens and publishing records are stored locally on the operator's computer. The token file is restricted to the operator's user account. The app has no server or database of its own.

Sharing

We do not sell or share this data. Videos, captions and access tokens are sent to Meta in order to provide the publishing function. Meta handles data under its own privacy policy. This web page is served by a hosting provider, which may keep standard access logs.

Retention and deletion

Local data is kept while the app is in use. The operator can delete it at any time and can revoke the app's access in Facebook settings. If you believe the app holds data about you and want it deleted, email us at the address below.

Changes

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new date.
